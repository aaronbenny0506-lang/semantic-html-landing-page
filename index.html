A garden for the whole neighborhood

Grow something real, together, one raised bed at a time.

Terra & Thyme turns an unused city lot into shared growing space — plots you can rent, workshops that teach you to use them well, and volunteer days that keep the whole garden thriving.

Reserve a plot See our programs

Why the garden exists

We started with one vacant lot and a handful of neighbors who wanted somewhere to grow food. Six seasons later, Terra & Thyme is a member-run collective: everyone who rents a bed also helps keep the shared paths, tools, and compost running.

64 growing beds tended by members
12 free workshops held each year
1,100 lb produce donated to the community pantry last season

Three ways to get your hands in the soil

Pick whichever fits how much time and space you have right now — you can always switch later.

Plot rental

A 4×8 raised bed of your own, water access, and a shared tool shed. Beds are assigned seasonally and include a starter soil test.

Workshop series

Monthly sessions on composting, seed starting, and season extension, taught by long-time members. Free for garden members, small fee for guests.

Volunteer days

No bed required. Show up on the first Saturday of the month to help with shared beds, compost turning, and pantry harvests.
