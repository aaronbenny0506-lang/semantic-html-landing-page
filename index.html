About the Garden

Terra & Thyme is a community garden collective. We turned an unused city lot into shared growing space with plots you can rent, workshops that teach you to use them well, and volunteer days that keep the garden thriving.

Our Programs

Plot Rental

A raised bed of your own, water access, and a shared tool shed.

Workshop Series

Monthly sessions on composting, seed starting, and season extension.

Volunteer Days

No bed required. Join us on the first Saturday of every month.
